Our Story

Built on word of mouth, one patient at a time

Exhale was founded by osteopath Hafi Rahman with a simple idea: give people the time to be properly listened to, a clear explanation of what's going on, and treatment that actually gets them better.

Most of our patients come to us through a friend or family member. Today our team of around ten osteopaths, physiotherapists and therapists works across two clinics — Exhale in Sutton Coldfield, and Lichfield Osteopathic Practice, which has been caring for patients in Lichfield since 1983 — with the same approach in both.
