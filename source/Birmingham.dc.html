Where We Are

In Sutton Coldfield — not the city centre

We want to be upfront: Exhale isn’t in central Birmingham. Our clinic is in Sutton Coldfield, a town within the City of Birmingham’s boundary, on its northern side. For many patients in north Birmingham that makes us a convenient choice; others are happy to travel a little further for the care they want.

If you’re weighing up whether the trip is worth it, call us on 0121 751 2440. We’ll talk through your problem and be honest if we think a clinic closer to you would suit you better.
